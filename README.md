## 🇬🇧 English

Generates short videos in a silkscreen decorative illustration style with a luxury creative-ad feel: fine even Ligne Claire line work, high-density narrative ornament, ivory or lime-white grounds, restrained warm-cool gem tones, and a fixed wide establishing shot paired with local close-follow shots. Audio keeps only natural sound effects that stay strictly synchronized with on-screen action — no BGM, no narration, no dialogue, no subtitles. The flow starts with theme confirmation, then produces Final_Video_Spec.md, a storyboard, key_element art, per-shot video, precise sound effects and the final assembly. Scope is original, non-branded illustration: it borrows a broad style language and never recreates specific logos or in-market patterns.

### ✨ What it does

- Theme to Final_Video_Spec.md, locking aspect ratio, runtime, style, language and audio policy
- Reference images analyzed for safely generalizable style and design evidence only
- Dual-storyboard narrative structure with key_element art per scene
- Ligne Claire even line work plus high-density decorative composition rules
- Per-shot video generation followed by precisely synced in-frame sound effects and final assembly
